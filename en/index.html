About me

Beyond the data

I'm a data scientist with a background in statistics. What I enjoy most about my work is finding ways to use data and apply statistical methods to turn it into information that helps people make decisions.

I also enjoy working with people who know the business and are willing to share that context. Understanding how they work, what they need and what they expect from the results helps me choose better methods, identify which information matters and build solutions that truly answer their needs.

I like exploring different methods and learning from the variety of problems that can be tackled with data. In the end, something I value about this profession is that data science can be applied in many sectors, as long as there is a real need and a willingness to work together.
